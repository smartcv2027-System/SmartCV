- Collaborate with Agile / Scrum development teams utilizing strong Problem Solving, Adaptability, and Communication capabilities.

REQUIREMENTS & QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or Software Engineering.
- Proven experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD Pipelines, and Linux administration.
- Hands-on familiarity with Cloud Computing platforms including AWS and Microsoft Azure.
- Working knowledge of Python scripting, SQL, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, and Git / GitHub.
- Solid understanding of Cybersecurity Basics, Problem Solving, Team Collaboration, and Communication.
