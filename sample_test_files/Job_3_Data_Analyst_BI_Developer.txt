Data Analyst & Business Intelligence Developer

- Partner with business stakeholders to gather reporting requirements and translate performance metrics into actionable recommendations.
- Maintain versioned analytics scripts using Git / GitHub and uphold high standards of Problem Solving, Time Management, and Team Collaboration.

REQUIREMENTS & QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, or Data Science.
- Advanced proficiency in SQL, PostgreSQL, Database Design, and Excel.
- Proven experience building interactive dashboards with Power BI, Tableau, and Data Visualization techniques.
- Strong background in Python, Data Analysis, Scikit-Learn, and Jupyter Notebooks.
- Excellent Problem Solving, Communication, Time Management, and Team Collaboration capabilities.
